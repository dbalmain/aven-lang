### String contract

- Existing ordinary double-quoted literals remain compatible.
- Ordinary triple quotes process escapes and `${...}` interpolation.
- Raw `r"..."` and raw triple quotes process neither.
- Raw hashes are included **now**, not reserved: `r#"..."#`, arbitrary matching
  hash counts, and the corresponding raw triple forms.
- Triple opening delimiter must be immediately followed by a physical newline.
- Before the closing delimiter on its line there must be only spaces. Ordinary
  expression syntax may follow the closing delimiter, e.g. a call's `)`.
- N spaces before the closer set the margin. Remove N leading spaces from every
  nonblank content line; insufficient indentation is an error.
- Whitespace-only lines with fewer than N leading spaces become empty. With at
  least N spaces, strip N and preserve excess whitespace. Tabs after the margin
  are payload; a tab-indented closer is invalid.
- Exclude the two boundary newlines. No implicit final LF; an extra blank
  content line requests one.
- Normalize physical CRLF and CR to LF before escape processing. Explicit `\r`
  in an ordinary literal still yields a carriage return.
- Formatter moves content and closing margin together, preserving values,
  including nested literals/interpolations and surrounding expression layout.
